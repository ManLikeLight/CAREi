---
name: Close to Home launch boundary
description: Product scope and approvals required before real Close to Home data
---

Close to Home launches with Trusted People Access Levels plus Today's Care (including Today's Reassurance), Who Was There?, Daily Story, and I'm Worried. Features outside that list require a separate written CAREi product decision.

**Why:** The user's Close to Home MVP Build Spec explicitly limits launch scope; competitor features do not justify expansion.

**How to apply:** Do not add family billing, preferences, live tracking, clinical AI, native/offline trusted-person apps, memories, or break-glass access as incidental improvements.

Keep data fictional until the DPIA and consent/authority model are approved, carer device security is live, Google Cloud London hosting and automated backups are confirmed, Azure regional UK South Standard routing is verified, and a named agency concern responder has configured deadlines.

**Why:** The specification makes these prerequisites for any real client or trusted-person data, not optional future enhancements.

**How to apply:** A working Replit sample release does not satisfy pilot hosting or approval gates. Do not connect legacy care records without verified geofence/QR and fully synced provenance.
